Features

Everything a tradesperson needs

Built for the way you really work: on site, on your phone, in a hurry.

🎙️

Voice quotation

Say the client's name and the items with prices. JobPay fills in the quotation, makes a PDF and opens WhatsApp to send it.

📄

Estimates & invoices

Quick rough estimates, firm quotations and professional invoices with your business details. Turn a quotation into an invoice in one tap.

⏱️

Job timer with pay

Set your amount per hour, tap Start, and watch your earnings grow. See your total for the day at a glance.

🧾

Receipt scanner

Take a photo of a receipt and the vendor, date and amount are read for you. Track expenses without typing.

📈

Earnings & profit

See what you earned this week, month or year, what is unpaid and what you spent, so you know your real profit.

👥

Clients, jobs & schedule

Keep your clients, jobs and calendar in one place, with the full history of every client.

📶

Works offline

No network on site? Keep working. Your data saves on your phone and syncs when you are back online.

📲

Install like an app

Add JobPay to your home screen from your browser. No app store, no big download.
