Privacy Policy
Effective Date: November 15, 2026  ·  Last Updated: November 15, 2026

Summary: JobPay keeps your business data on your phone and in Firebase. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. You can delete your account and data any time. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

1. Who We Are

JobPay ("we", "us", "our") is a business app for tradespeople in Nigeria and beyond. It helps you create quotations, estimates and invoices, track jobs and time, scan receipts and see your earnings. JobPay is run as an independent service from Nigeria and is the data controller for the personal data of its users.

Questions or requests about your data: Javascriipt01@gmail.com

2. What We Collect

  • Account details: your email address, phone number or Google account name and email, depending on how you sign in. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and we cannot see them.
  • Profile: your name, trade, business name, address, hourly rate and optional profile photo.
  • Business data you enter: clients (names, phone numbers, emails, addresses), quotations, estimates, invoices, jobs, schedule, expenses, fuel logs, time logs, voice-note text, receipt photos, notes and signatures.
  • Verification data: when you sign up or reset a password we create a 6-digit code. We keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it for 10 minutes so we can check it. We do not store the code itself.
  • Device and technical data: a copy of your data kept on your device (browser storage), and basic error reports (see Sentry below).

We do not collect your bank card details. If you use online payment links for your clients, the payment provider handles card data and we only get confirmation that payment was made.

3. Why We Use It

  • to create and secure your account, and to send you verification and password-reset codes;
  • to run the app: save and sync your data, produce PDFs and show your totals;
  • to read receipts and voice notes when you ask us to;
  • to find and fix errors, and to keep the Service safe;
  • to contact you about your account or important service changes, and to meet legal duties.

Our legal bases under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 are: your consent (when you sign up and when you use the scanner or voice features), the need to provide the Service you asked for, our legitimate interest in keeping the Service secure and working, and legal obligations.

4. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your personal data and we do not show ads. We use these service providers, who process data for us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): sign-in and cloud storage of your account and business data.
  • Vercel: hosts the app and runs our server functions.
  • Brevo (email provider): receives your email address and the message so it can deliver your verification and reset codes. We may change to another email provider, and will update this page.
  • Google Gemini: if you use the Receipt Scanner, the photo is sent through our server to Gemini to read the vendor, date and amount. We do not keep the photo on our server. On Google's free tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products, so do not scan anything private.
  • Your browser's speech service (Google in Chrome): turns your voice into text for Voice Notes and Quotation. It may process audio online.
  • Sentry: receives error reports (what went wrong in the app) so we can fix bugs.
  • Google Fonts: delivers fonts, so Google can see your IP address when fonts load.
  • Payment providers (such as Paystack), only if you or your clients use online payment links.
  • Authorities, when the law, a court or a regulator requires it, or to protect our rights and users.

When you send a quotation, estimate or invoice to a client (for example by WhatsApp or email), that is your own action. Whoever you send it to receives it under your control.

5. Where Your Data Is Kept and Transfers Abroad

Your data is kept on your device and, when you are signed in and online, in Google Firebase. The providers above may process data on servers outside Nigeria. We choose providers that use security measures such as encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, and we take steps so that your data is protected when it is transferred, as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 requires.

6. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • We keep your data while your account is open.
  • You can delete your account in Settings with Delete my account. This removes your account and the data we hold for you in the cloud, and clears the copy on your device. You can also email us to ask.
  • Backups kept by our providers are removed on their normal schedules.
  • Verification codes (hashed) expire after 10 minutes.
  • We may keep limited records where the law requires it or to settle a dispute.

7. Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you have the right to:

  • know what data we hold about you and get a copy (you can also use Backup Data in Settings);
  • correct data that is wrong (you can edit most of it in the app);
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • ask us to limit how we use it, or object to certain uses;
  • take your data to another service (data portability);
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before;
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you think we handled your data wrongly.

Email us to use any of these rights. We aim to reply within 30 days.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, Firebase Authentication, hashed verification codes that expire quickly, a limit on wrong code attempts, and keep our secret keys on the server and not in the app. No system is perfectly safe, so please use a strong password, never share your codes, and log out on shared phones. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the NDPC within the time the law requires.

9. Permissions on Your Phone

The app may ask for your microphone (voice notes and voice quotations), camera or photos (receipts, job photos, profile picture) and location (GPS job features). You can say no, and the rest of the app still works. You can change permissions at any time in your phone or browser settings.

10. Local Storage

JobPay uses your browser's local storage and a service worker to work offline and to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies or ad trackers.

11. Your Clients' Data

When you add your clients' details, you decide how they are used. You are responsible for having the right to keep them and for telling your clients how you use them. For that data, you are the controller and we process it for you to provide the Service.

12. Children

JobPay is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will put the new version here with a new date, and tell you in the app or by email if the change is important.
