Privacy Policy
Effective Date: June 1, 2025  ·  Last Updated: June 1, 2025

Summary: Java Vault stores your business data securely on your device and optionally in Firebase. We do not sell your data. We do not show you ads. Your data belongs to you.

1. Who We Are

Java Vault ("we", "us", "our") is a business management application built for tradespeople — plumbers, electricians, HVAC technicians, and other skilled trades professionals. Java Vault is operated as an independent service.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: support@javavault.app

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use Java Vault:

  • Account Information: Your email address, phone number, or Google account details when you create an account or log in.
  • Business Data: Estimates, invoices, job records, client details, expenses, inventory, photos, and any other information you enter into the app.
  • Device Data: A unique device identifier stored locally to manage your session and offline data.
  • Usage Data: Basic information about how the app is used, such as which sections are visited, to improve the product. This data is not linked to your identity.
  • Payment Data: When you process payments through Paystack or Flutterwave, those providers handle your payment information. We only receive confirmation of successful payments, not your card details.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the Java Vault application and its features
  • To sync your data across devices via Firebase Cloud Firestore
  • To send you invoices and estimates as you request
  • To process subscription payments and manage your plan
  • To improve the application based on aggregate usage patterns
  • To contact you about your account or service updates (only when necessary)

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in two places:

  • Locally on your device: Your browser's localStorage holds a copy of your data for offline access.
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore: When you are signed in and online, your data syncs to Google Firebase, which uses industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit.

We use Firebase Authentication for account security. Passwords are never stored in plain text. All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Firebase / Google: For data storage and authentication. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Paystack: For payment processing in Nigeria and Africa. Subject to Paystack's Privacy Policy.
  • Flutterwave: For payment processing. Subject to Flutterwave's Privacy Policy.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect our rights.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: Export all your data using the "Backup Data" feature in settings.
  • Delete your data: Use "Reset App" in settings to permanently delete all your local data. Contact us to delete your Firebase data.
  • Correct your data: Edit any information directly in the app at any time.
  • Data portability: Your data can be exported as a JSON file at any time.
  • Withdraw consent: You can stop using the service and delete your account at any time.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

Java Vault uses browser localStorage (not cookies) to store your app data and session information. No advertising cookies or tracking pixels are used. The app does not use third-party analytics or advertising networks.

8. Children's Privacy

Java Vault is designed for business use by adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

9. Client Data You Enter

When you add your clients' details to Java Vault (names, phone numbers, emails, addresses), you are responsible for ensuring you have the right to store this information and that your clients are aware of how their data is used. You should include reference to Java Vault in any privacy policy you have with your own clients.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of Java Vault after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
