Refund Policy
Effective Date: November 15, 2026  ·  Last Updated: November 15, 2026

Summary: JobPay is free right now, so there is nothing to pay and nothing to refund. If we add paid features later, we will tell you 30 days before, show every price up front, and publish clear refund rules first.

1. JobPay Is Free Right Now

JobPay is currently free for everyone. We do not take any payment from you for using the app, so there is nothing to refund or cancel. There is no free trial and no subscription to end.

2. If We Add Paid Features Later

We may add paid features or plans in the future. If we do:

  • we will tell you in the app and by email at least 30 days before anything you use today starts to cost money;
  • you will always see the price before you pay, in Nigerian Naira (₦), with no hidden fees;
  • you will only be charged after you choose a paid option and confirm it;
  • you will be able to cancel at any time inside the app, and keep what you paid for until the end of that period;
  • we will refund duplicate charges, wrong amounts, and charges taken after you cancelled in time;
  • we will publish the full billing and refund rules on this page before the first payment is taken.

3. Payments Between You and Your Clients

If you use online payment links to collect money from your clients, that payment is between you and your client. JobPay is not a party to it and does not hold the money. Refunds to a client are your responsibility, and you should agree them with your client. Payment providers such as Paystack have their own rules.

4. Problems With a Charge

If you ever see a charge that you do not recognise and that you think comes from JobPay, email us with the date, amount and payment reference. We reply within 3 business days.
