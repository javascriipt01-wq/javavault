Refund and Cancellation Policy
Effective Date: November 2, 2026  ·  Last Updated: November 2, 2026

Summary: Every new account gets a 7-day free trial with no card needed. Paid plans renew every month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time and keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for. We refund duplicate charges and billing errors.

1. Free Trial

New accounts get 7 days of Pro access free. We do not ask for a card during the trial and we do not charge you when it ends. When the trial ends your account moves to the Free plan unless you choose a paid plan.

2. Paid Plans and Renewal

  • Plans are billed monthly in Nigerian Naira (₦) through Paystack.
  • A plan renews automatically every month until you cancel.
  • We show the price before you pay. There are no hidden fees.

3. How to Cancel

Open Java Vault, go to Subscription & Plans and cancel your plan. Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep your paid features until the end of the month you already paid for. After that your account moves to the Free plan and your data stays safe.

4. When We Give a Refund

  • Duplicate charge: you were charged twice for the same period.
  • Billing error: you were charged a wrong amount, or after you cancelled before the renewal date.
  • Service failure: you paid and the paid features did not turn on, and we could not fix it within 7 days.
  • The law: any refund you are entitled to under Nigerian consumer protection law.

5. When We Do Not Give a Refund

We do not refund a month that has already been delivered because you stopped using the app or forgot to cancel. You can avoid this by cancelling before the renewal date.

6. How to Ask for a Refund

Email us within 7 days of the charge. Include the email you used to sign up, the date, the amount and the Paystack payment reference. We reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Paystack and usually reach you in 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

7. Chargebacks

Please contact us first. We can usually solve a billing problem faster than a bank dispute.
